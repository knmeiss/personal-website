July 29, 2026

Build an Agent Harness for Your React Native App

Chain React • Portland, OR

A hands-on workshop with Giovanni Laquidara and Kourtney Meiss. "Stop writing prompts. Start building the loop that writes them for you." You'll construct a harness of focused agents that plan, implement, and verify each other's work, backed by reusable tools, project memory, and checkpoints. Starting from a single agent, you'll build up to a working pipeline you can run against your own app. The workshop uses TV as the live target, since it's a genuinely non-trivial adaptation with focus navigation, 10-foot layouts, remote input, and multiple OS targets, but the techniques apply to whatever you're building. As a final layer, you'll add a context-aware agent powered by Bee that records your conversations about the app and feeds them back into the harness when it runs. You'll leave with a forkable repo and a clear mental model for building development systems instead of babysitting prompts.

Keeping Up Without Burning Out: My AI Workflow for DevRel

DevRelCon NYC • Industry City, Brooklyn, NY

The pace of AI development feels overwhelming. New tools, techniques, and paradigms emerge weekly. As someone driving AI adoption at Amazon while trying to stay current myself, I've developed a personal workflow and mindset that helps me keep up without burning out. In this lightning talk, I'll share my current toolbox, the questions I ask myself that help me level up, and how I decide when to use AI vs. do things myself to actually learn. This isn't about having all the answers. It's about building a sustainable approach to working in a field that moves faster than any of us can follow.

Performance First Thinking: Lessons from Vega OS

React Native London

Building React Native applications on hardware-constrained devices requires deliberate development choices from day one. We'll explore why we built a performance-optimized operating system designed specifically for Amazon devices (Vega OS), and the unique architecture that powers it. You'll learn from the real-world challenges we faced and solutions for building apps within low-memory environments. Whether you are building for mobile, web, or TVs, you'll walk away with practical optimization techniques you can apply to your own development.
